# 13 — Vibe-Coder Social Platform

> Founder direction: *make it a social platform for vibe coders to communicate.*

This is the growth and retention flywheel, not a side feature. The vibe-coder audience is
**tightly networked** (Indie Hackers, X build-in-public, Discords) and research showed
community channels — especially specificity-driven ones like Indie Hackers — are the most
effective way to reach them (`10` §3). **Owning the watering hole** turns one-time tool users
into a daily-active community and creates the demand-side that makes the verified badge matter.

## 1. Why a social layer (the strategic logic)

1. **Distribution & retention.** Builders come for the free tools (readiness, verified revenue,
   deadline alerts), stay for the community — daily reasons to return, not just at a raise.
2. **The verified badge needs a stage.** A "verified $X ARR / capital-ready" badge only becomes
   a pull signal if people *see* it. A social feed is where it's displayed, shared, and envied
   (the SOC-2 / TrustMRR dynamic — `06`/`09`).
3. **Two-sided network effect.** Builders attract capital providers (deal flow); providers'
   presence attracts builders. The community is where the `12` capital network comes alive.
4. **Data flywheel.** Engagement + verified profiles improve matching, the proprietary data
   reports that win GEO (`10` §2b), and the "who's building/raising what" signal.

## 2. Core features

| Surface | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Profiles** | Builder profile with **verified** badges (revenue, readiness, audit-ready), stack, what they're building/raising |
| **Build-in-public feed** | Post updates, milestones, MRR moves — auto-postable from the verified spine ("just crossed $5k verified MRR") |
| **Verified revenue leaderboard** | TrustMRR-style, but tied to the real product — proof-of-traction that ranks and pulls |
| **Capital corner** | Share where you are in a raise (opt-in), what you're looking for; matched providers (`12`) participate |
| **Ask / Q&A** | "How do I file my 83(b)?" "Which lender for $8k MRR?" — answered by community + the AI copilot, surfacing product value |
| **Find co-founders / talent / contractors** | The networked audience's recurring need; ties to the cap-table/equity tooling |
| **Groups / topics** | By stack (Lovable, Cursor), stage, sector (AI), geography |
| **Templates & wins gallery** | Shareable starter kits (feeds the `11` scaffolding distribution) |

## 3. The AI copilot is a community member
The capital-readiness copilot (`02` A0 / `11` skill) lives *in* the feed: it answers questions,
nudges deadlines publicly (with consent), and turns community questions into product
activation ("I can check that for you — connect Stripe"). This is organic, in-context
conversion no ad can match.

## 4. Network effects & moat
- **Builder side:** community + free tools + verified badge → retention and word-of-mouth.
- **Provider side:** verified, pre-qualified deal flow → providers join → more reason for
  builders to be present and verified.
- **Standard-setting:** as the badge spreads, *not* having it becomes a red flag — the pull
  that made SOC 2 table-stakes. The social platform is the mechanism that manufactures the
  buyer-side forcing function `06` said was the make-or-break.

## 5. Hard parts (don't gloss them)
- **Cold start.** Communities are hard and slow. Seed with build-in-public power users and the
  free deadline-alert/verified-revenue hooks (a reason to show up before the community exists).
  Borrow audiences (Indie Hackers, X) before owning one.
- **Don't let it become spam-to-investors.** The instant providers are present, protect them:
  intros are **opt-in and verified-data-gated** (via `12`), never open cold-DM spam. This is
  also why neutrality + managed intros matter — uncontrolled outreach destroys the provider
  side.
- **Moderation & trust.** Verified profiles reduce fakery; clear anti-spam, anti-fraud, and
  content policies. The "verified" identity is itself a moderation asset.
- **Privacy.** Sharing revenue/raise status is **opt-in**; the neutrality firewall (`12`) means
  we never expose a user's data without consent.

## 6. Monetization tie-in (don't monetize the conversation directly)
The community is the funnel, not the meter. Monetize the **edges**: premium intros (`12`),
recruiting/talent, the capital-readiness subscription (`09`), and provider-paid verified deal
flow — while keeping the conversation free and the data neutral.

## 7. Build sequencing
1. **Hook first, community second:** ship the free verified-revenue + readiness + deadline
   tools and the shareable badge/score (a reason to post) before the full social product.
2. **Lightweight feed + profiles + leaderboard** built on verified data (differentiated from
   day one because the numbers are *verified*, not self-claimed).
3. **Q&A + copilot-in-feed**, then **groups** and **capital corner** (gated, opt-in) once
   there's a provider side from `12`.
4. **Templates/wins gallery** to feed the `11` scaffolding distribution loop.

> The social platform is the demand-generation and standard-setting engine for everything else;
> keep it free, verified, neutral, and protected from spam — its value is trust, and trust is
> the whole company.
