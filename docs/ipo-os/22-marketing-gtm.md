# 22 — Marketing & GTM (VCOP)

> Executable marketing/GTM plan. **The wedge isn't "we lend money" — it's "we make you
> financeable."** Debt is the payoff, not the hook.

## Core narrative
*"You can ship a product in a weekend now. You still can't get a dollar of capital for it. VCOP
gives every AI builder verified financials and a credit score from day one — so the moment you
have revenue, you have access to capital. No spreadsheets, no data room, no pitch deck. Connect
read-only, get verified, get funded."*

Position against the status quo: Brex/Ramp = for *funded* startups; banks = paperwork hell; RBF =
opaque/slow/salesy. Own the **pre-seed-of-everything** moment: the solo builder with $400 MRR who
is invisible to every financial system.

## Segments & messages
- **A. Shipping indie hacker** (primary, $0–10k MRR, build-in-public): *"Your Stripe says you're
  real. Now make it official — verified revenue + a credit score from day one, and instant growth
  capital when you scale. Free to start."*
- **B. Vibe-coding-for-clients agency** (irregular cash flow): *"Stop fronting cloud bills and ad
  spend. Audited cash flow + a line of growth debt that moves at the speed you ship."*
- **C. Almost-a-startup micro-SaaS** ($5–50k MRR): *"Skip the dilution detour. A verified record +
  non-dilutive growth debt off your real numbers. We also keep you out of trouble on 83(b)/BOI."*

## Value props (ranked)
1. Instant, paperwork-free growth debt off audited data (the money). 2. Verified "Verified Builder"
badge (viral, free, status). 3. Credit score from day one (gamified, weekly return). 4. Auditable
always-current financials. 5. Deadline alerts (83(b)/BOI/cap-table — the "saved my ass" share).
6. Agent-native (MCP) access ("manage your startup's money inside Claude").

## Objection handling
- **"Is my data safe?"** → *Read-only by design — OAuth, no stored creds, can't move money,
  revoke in one click.* Lead with **read-only** everywhere; plain-English security page + SOC 2
  roadmap + pen-test badge.
- **"Just another lender?"** → *Debt is optional and last, not the pitch. The verified-financials
  layer is free forever and useful alone. We show the rate before you click.*
- **"Why trust a score you invented?"** → *Built only from your real connected data, auditable,
  factors shown — a checklist of what makes you financeable, and it travels with you.*
- **"I'm too small."** → *That's the point. Day one is when we start; track record is the asset.*

**Why now:** the vibe-coding boom created millions of revenue-generating builders with **zero
financial infrastructure built for them**; MCP made it possible to distribute finance *inside the
tools they build in*; build-in-public spreads verification badges at near-zero CAC.

## Hooks (free, day-one, shareable)
Verified Revenue **badge** (embeddable, unfakeable, referral-linked) · **deadline alerts** (83(b)
30-day window etc. — altruistic shares) · **TrustScore** (gamified "540 → 690" posts) · **"are you
financeable yet?"** check. Every free artifact is an OG-image-optimized, branded, referral-tagged
unit. *The product is the marketing.*

## Launch plan (8 weeks)
- **W1–4 (pre-launch):** waitlist + landing (single value prop); founder daily build-in-public;
  read-only security page; ship badge to 20–30 alpha builders (seed "Verified Builder #1…30"); tease
  deadline-alert hook; ship MCP to alpha + record the "inside Claude" Loom; line up 10–15 PH
  supporters; finalize PH assets.
- **W5 (launch):** PH Tue/Wed 12:01am PT (reply to every comment; push waitlist 6am/9am/noon) +
  Show HN (technical framing) + X launch thread (pinned) + one value-first Reddit post.
- **W6–8 (post):** publish **"State of Vibe-Coder Revenue"** report → press + dev newsletters; open
  the **debt** path to qualified users → "$X in 10 min, no paperwork" case studies; launch
  leaderboard + referral; second-wave (Stripe Marketplace listing, first template, partner-Discord AMA).

## Growth loops
1. **Verified-badge loop** (primary): connect → badge → embed on site/X/IH → followers click →
   connect (each badge is a referral link; the "powered by" loop applied to status).
2. **Leaderboard loop** (opt-in verified-MRR / score climbers → status → signups → feeds data
   reports). 3. **Referral** tied to real value (better debt terms / fee credit per referred Verified
   Builder + leaderboard clout). 4. **Data flywheel**: more builders → richer dataset → better
   model + underwriting → faster offers → testimonials → more builders. **The moat is the data.**

**Reach → free-hook activation (connect read-only) → habit (check score/alerts) → subscribe → debt
→ advocate (badge + referral).** North-star: **verified, connected builders with live revenue
data.**

  cadence; double down on the highest-K-factor loop.
