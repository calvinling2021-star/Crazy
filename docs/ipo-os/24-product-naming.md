## Recommended: **Attestly**
*Alternatives: **Capline** (safe/literal), **Attesta** (premium/coined).*

### Why Attestly
- **It is the strategy.** The wedge is *verified, auditable financials that underwrite capital*
  — "Attestly" means *attested*. Competitors lend; Attestly **attests**, and capital follows
  ("the key is seamless, not the fund").
- **Verbs into the core action:** *"Connect Stripe and **attest** your revenue."* A name that
  verbs becomes the trust primitive — perfect for a "verified, not vibes" brand.
- **Developer-native & typeable** (`attestly`, one spelling), sits in the respected
  **trust/audit-infrastructure** lane (Vanta's neighborhood) — not the crowded lender row
  (Capchase/Pipe/Arc) or the -pay/-fi/-fund cliché soup.
- **Researched clean** (directional): no "Attestly" company surfaced; coined enough to own a
  `.com` + a mark, real enough to parse instantly. **Watch-item:** the differently-spelled
  *Attest* (askattest.com, research SaaS) in a different class — clear in §7 of the report.

### Winner package
- **Taglines:** primary **"Verified, not vibes."** · product **"Attest your revenue. Get your
  capital."** · manifesto **"The key is seamless, not the fund."**
- **Wordmark:** lowercase `attestly`, geometric-mono/clean grotesk, a subtle check/seal glyph;
  monochrome-first + one "verified" accent. No gradients/coins/arrows.
- **Branded-house sub-architecture:** **Attestly Connect** (read-only rails) → **Attestly
  Standing** (the credit-from-day-one score) → **Attestly Line** (instant growth capital — a
  *line*, not a "fund"), all callable via **Attestly MCP** (`attestly-mcp`). In-agent verb:
  *"attest my last 90 days."*

### Naming architecture
- **VCOP stays internal** — never customer-facing; retire at launch, keep in repos/decks.
- **"Vibe coder" = audience copy, never the brand name.** "Vibe" actively fights a
  verified-not-vibes / Sage trust brand, and the term will date. Good: *"Built for vibe coders
  and indie AI builders."* Bad: *VibeLedger / VibeCap.*
- **Branded house:** one master brand + plain descriptive sub-names (Connect/Standing/Line/MCP);
  devs dislike cute sub-brands.
