# 18 — Competitor Landscape

> Global scan of who else is building toward our space, from deep research across two axes:
> (A) verified-data → capital / capital-readiness / diligence automation, and (B) embedded/RBF
> lending off connected data + startup credit + AI finance back office. Sources cited inline.

## Headline
**The full loop is unbuilt; the pieces have incumbents.** No one offers a neutral, founder-owned,
audit-grade verification layer that feeds *both* debt and equity from one continuously-true
profile — and critically, **"credit from day one off audited books" is genuinely unoccupied.**
But the wedge is surrounded: RBF lenders (revenue-gated), AI bookkeeping (no lending), card
issuers (credit off cash balance), and embedded-lending infra all own adjacent links.

## Most-direct competitors (and why each falls short of the thesis)

| Competitor | HQ | What | Gap vs our thesis |
|---|---|---|---|

> The closest competitors all underwrite off *existing recurring revenue* and target $250k–$1M+
> ARR. **None underwrites a day-one AI builder off audited books + a from-scratch credit
> history.** That exact wedge is unoccupied.

## "Credit from day one off audited data" — the clearest whitespace
- **Ramp / Brex** — card credit off **cash balance**, no founder credit check (Brex ~$50k min cash,
  Ramp ~$25k). Nearest "credit off your financials," but it's **card spend limits keyed to cash**,
  not growth debt or a portable score. (Ramp $750M at $8.1B, 2025; Brex ~$12B.)
- **Parafin** — turns connected platform/payment data into instant SMB offers (DoorDash, Amazon);
  >$100M ARR, $360M forward-flow from Cross River. **B2B infra for marketplaces, not a founder-
  facing startup credit bureau.**
- **Stripe Capital** — advances off Stripe processing data; Stripe-locked, MCA-shaped.
- **No genuine "startup credit bureau."** No D&B-equivalent scores pre-/low-revenue startups off
  audited ledgers; lenders fall back to the founder's **personal FICO**. This is the single
  clearest open primitive: a **portable, founder-owned "FICO for startups" seeded from day one.**

## The adjacent layers (partner or forward-integration threat)
- **AI finance back office** (produces the "audited data"): **Puzzle** ($50M, "audit-ready" books,
  most thesis-relevant), **Digits** ($565M val, autonomous GL), **Campfire** ($65M B, AI ERP),
  Pilot, Aleph (FP&A). They make audit-ready books **but don't lend** — partners, or threats if
  they add credit.
- **Audit automation** (the "audited" guarantee): **DataSnipper** ($1B, all Big Four), MindBridge,
  Trullion, FloQast. Credibility layer; **none links audit output to credit issuance.**
- **Embedded-lending infra / marketplaces** (buy-vs-build the rail): **Lendflow** (75+ lenders +
  "Intelligence" scoring, Experian), **Lendio**, **Parafin**, **Liberis**, **YouLend**.
- **Global RBF** (all revenue-gated, balance-sheet): US (Lighter, Clearco), EU (Uncapped, Silvr
  €148M, Karmen, Wayflyer >$2B disbursed, Outfund, Levenue, re:cap), India/SEA (GetVantage, Klub,
  Velocity, Jenfi, Choco Up).

## Dead / pivoted / distressed (the cautionary graveyard)
**Pipe** (pivoted 2023 to embedded infra; ~$7.1M rev on $47M burn 2024; ~50% layoffs, CEO churn
2025) · **Parker** (bankruptcy May 2026 despite $200M+ raised) · **Cushion** (shut Jan 2025) ·
**Fundid** (shut 2024) · **Bench** (shut Dec 2024) · **Clearco** (heavy down-rounds) · EU RBF
cohort broadly stressed 2022–23 (Sifted). Lesson: revenue-purchase/RBF and e-comm-card models are
fragile; the durable edge is being the **source of truth**, not just another lender.

## The consolidated whitespace (what we could own)
1. **Credit from day one off audited books** — a portable, founder-owned startup credit score/
   history, seeded the moment they connect Stripe/bank/ledger. **Unoccupied.**
2. **Bundle the audited-books engine WITH the lender** — Puzzle/Digits make books but don't lend;
   Capchase/Founderpath lend but trust 3rd-party data. **We produce the audited data AND underwrite
   on it** → collapses two products, closes the trust gap. The vertical-integration moat both sides
   would have to build.
3. **The vibe-coder / AI-builder segment is unserved by capital** — huge 2025 flows into the
   *tools* (Lovable $6.6B, Cursor, Replit) but **zero financing primitives for the micro-startups
   built on them.** Stripe-first, clean digital trails, too small for Capchase/VD. First mover owns
   a brand-new exploding cohort.
4. **"Paperwork-free *because* we're the source of truth"** — "instant" alone isn't differentiation
   (Capchase = 30s). The wedge is *nothing to submit* because we already hold the audited books.
5. **Global, portable verified profile** — every strong analog is regional; a cross-border verified
   trust profile doesn't exist.
6. **(Later) equity + debt from one verified profile** — marketplaces are debt-only; equity tools
   ignore connected data. The unified "raise OR borrow from the same verified profile" is empty.

## The real threats to move ahead of
Not the RBF lenders (revenue-gated; several dead/distressed). The threats are:
- **Ramp / Brex / Mercury** owning the **primary account** and bolting on day-one credit.
- **Back-office players (Puzzle / Digits)** adding lending off the books they already produce.
→ **Move fast on the score-from-audited-data primitive** before either side closes the gap.

## Net read against the reshape (`17`)
The competitor map **confirms the focused wedge**: the narrowed product — *verified/audited spine →
instant, paperwork-free growth debt + credit-from-day-one, for vibe coders* — sits in **genuine,
defensible whitespace**, while every *broad* surface we'd added (cap table, data room, diligence,
audit, IPO, social) has well-funded incumbents. Differentiation is strongest exactly where the
debate said to focus. Defensibility lives in the **portable credit-from-audited-data primitive +
vertical integration (books + lending)** — not in any single downstream feature.
