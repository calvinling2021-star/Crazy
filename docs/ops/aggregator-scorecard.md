## Directional read (confirm in diligence)
- **Lendflow — primary candidate.** Embedded-lending *infrastructure* + a 75+ lender marketplace +
  business scoring; best fit for neutral, white-label, agent-native multi-offer. Highest on
  criteria 1 & 3.
- **Lendio — strong secondary (panel depth).** SMB-loan marketplace ("one line of code"); good offer
  breadth; more broker-shaped/less API-native. Use as a second panel source to avoid concentration.
- **Parafin — optional supply, watch neutrality.** Embedded-finance *capital* provider that leans
  toward being the funder → risks the "are you favoring your own balance sheet?" perception. Only
  include as **one funder among many**, never the default.

## Non-negotiables (walk away if missing)
- They are the **funder-of-record** and own **state disclosure/registration** in writing.
- **No requirement** that VCOP quote/negotiate terms, complete the application, or take % of proceeds.
- We **retain ownership** of the deterministic spine + entity score.
- They support a **panel** (not single-funder lock-in).
